Design your own feature extractor

Build a CNN one decision at a time. Pick a mammogram patch, then choose each component and watch what it does to your patch. Lock your choice to unlock the next step. At the end, see how your design does, and change any step to compare.

Patches: Sawyer-Lee R, Gimenez F, Hoogi A, Rubin D. Curated Breast Imaging Subset of DDSM (CBIS-DDSM). The Cancer Imaging Archive, 2016. doi:10.7937/K9/TCIA.2016.7O02S9CY. CC BY 3.0. Architecture: Simonyan & Zisserman, "Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition," ICLR 2015. Model scores and block 5 maps come from the VGG19 trained for Workshop 2.
